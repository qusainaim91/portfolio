Jayeek Bil Haki

Landing page for a podcast where two friends talk through social issues as Middle Eastern women, using their own lives as the material.

2024 UX/UI & Frontend Development

One page, one scroll. Enough to say what the show is and who is talking, then get out of the way.

The name is Levantine for something close to “here comes the talk,” and the design takes it at its word. A blocky Arabic wordmark fills the hero. No photography, no icons. The show is two voices and a conversation, so the page is type and very little else.
